# ✏️ practice/

**This is your work, not the textbook.**

`reference/` contains the course material — teaching notes, annotated example
scripts, and animated diagrams I built from your four lecturer manuals.

**This folder is for the work *you* write.** Everything in here should be code
you created yourself, in your own words, as you work through the course.

## How to use it

- Do each Manual-1/2/3/4 lesson **here**, not in `reference/`
- Commit your own versions of the scripts as you build them
- Keep them numbered like the lessons, e.g.
  ```
  practice/01_databases/01-create-student-records-db.sql
  practice/02_tables/01-create-students.sql
  practice/02_tables/02-insert-sample-students.sql
  ```
- **Break things on purpose.** Change a type, drop a key, run a query you expect
  to fail — then read the error. That's where the learning happens.

## What goes here, what doesn't

| Put here | Keep out |
|---|---|
| SQL you wrote while learning | Copies of the textbook scripts, renamed |
| Your exercises and answers | Your lecturer's material verbatim |
| Notes you wrote yourself | Anything with a password or real personal data |

**No passwords, tokens, or real personal data — ever.** This repo is public.

## Why two folders?

`reference/` is the textbook. `practice/` is the homework. Keeping them separate
makes the history readable: when your lecturer or a recruiter looks, `practice/`
is what they should read first to see what you actually built.

> The two folders can coexist in one repo. You don't need a second repository.
